2notes, not audio

Change it later.

The app keeps the notes you played, so nothing is final. Quantize, swing, humanize, swap the instrument, or fix a note in the piano roll. Your original timing is always one tap away.

3capture mode

Perform it live.

Every layer becomes a pad. Tap to drop it out, tap again to bring it back. Hold and drag to bend it with an effect, hold still to lock it, and record the take.

Pick it up anywhere.

Start a loop on your phone, finish the song on your laptop. Share a project with a six-character code and open it on any device, your own sounds included. No account needed.

get started

Plug in. Play.

No account and nothing to install on a computer or Android. Your projects are saved in your browser.

Computer ready

Chrome or Edge on Mac, Windows or Linux. Connect the HiChord with a USB-C cable and open the app.

Android ready

Chrome, with the HiChord on USB-C. Use an OTG adapter if your phone needs one.

iPhone and iPad coming

iOS browsers can't talk to MIDI devices, so this needs a small app. It's built and on its way. Can't wait? Install it on your iPhone or iPad yourself with Xcode on a Mac: the steps are on GitHub.
